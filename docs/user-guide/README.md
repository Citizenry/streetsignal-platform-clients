# User Guide

Complete guide for end users on how to interact with the StreetSignal Telegram bot.

## Getting Started

The StreetSignal Telegram bot makes it easy to submit reports about issues in your community directly through Telegram. No need to visit websites or download apps - just chat with the bot!

## Quick Start

1. **Find the Bot**: Search for your community's StreetSignal bot in Telegram
2. **Start Chatting**: Send `/start` to begin
3. **Submit Reports**: Use `/report` to report community issues
4. **Get Help**: Send `/help` anytime for assistance

## What You Can Do

### 📝 Submit Reports
Report community issues like:
- Potholes and road damage
- Broken streetlights
- Graffiti and vandalism
- Illegal dumping
- Traffic signal problems
- Park maintenance issues

### 🔗 Link Your Account
Connect your StreetSignal account to:
- Track your submitted reports
- Receive updates on report status
- Access additional features

### 📊 View Your Reports
Check the status of reports you've submitted and see community activity.

## Getting Help

- **In-Bot Help**: Send `/help` to the bot anytime
- **Command List**: Send `/start` to see available options
- **Support**: Contact your local StreetSignal administrators

## Privacy and Safety

- **Anonymous Reports**: You can submit reports without linking an account
- **Data Privacy**: Your personal information is protected
- **Community Guidelines**: Be respectful and accurate in your reports
